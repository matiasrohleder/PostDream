# Preferences and your account

## Preferences

**Settings → Preferences** holds per-browser preferences.

![Preferences](/img/screenshots/preferences.png)

| Preference | Options |
|---|---|
| **Language** | English, Español, Deutsch. Auto-detected from the browser; also switchable in the top bar and on the login page. |
| **Theme** | **System** (follows your operating system), **Light** or **Dark**. |
| **Desktop notifications** | Notify about new emails while the tab is in the background. The browser asks for permission the first time; if notifications are blocked for the site, the page tells you. |

These choices are saved in the browser, not on the server.

## My account

**Settings → My account** lets you change your password (at least 8 characters). It is shown only when the UI login
is enabled.

![My account](/img/screenshots/account.png)

Passwords are stored with bcrypt. Admins can reset other users' passwords from
[Users](../administration/users-and-roles.md).
